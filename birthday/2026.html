03 / The bigger picture

Why I support the Alzheimer's Association.

My dad was diagnosed with Alzheimer's in 2017. He passed away in 2022. I've seen firsthand how devastating this disease can be for someone you love and for everyone who loves them.

For my birthday, I'm inviting you to join me for some of the games that have meant the most to me over the years—and, if you're able, to help support the work toward a future without Alzheimer's.

Thanks for being part of it. Whether you donate, watch, or spread the word, I really appreciate you.
